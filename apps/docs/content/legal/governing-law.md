## Arbitration - SIAC, seated in Singapore

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with a QNSI agreement - including any question about its existence, validity, or termination - shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) in accordance with the SIAC Rules in force at the time the notice of arbitration is submitted.

- Seat of arbitration: Singapore.
- Language of arbitration: English.
- Tribunal: one arbitrator, unless the parties agree otherwise.
- The award is final and binding on both parties.

## Why arbitration in Singapore is the neutral choice

Singapore is a New York Convention state, which means a SIAC award is enforceable in over 170 countries. For a customer outside Singapore this is usually a better outcome than litigating in either party's home courts: the forum is neutral, the process is private, and the award travels.

## Costs

Costs follow the event. The unsuccessful party bears the costs of the arbitration - including the tribunal's fees, SIAC's administrative fees, and the successful party's reasonable legal costs - to the extent the tribunal so determines under the SIAC Rules.
