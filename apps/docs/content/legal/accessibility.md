## Commitment

QNSI aims to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA across the public website, the developer documentation, and the customer portal. Public-sector and regulated buyers are frequently required to procure only from vendors that make this commitment, and we take it as a product requirement, not a checkbox.

## What we build to

- Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and landmark regions.
- Keyboard operability for interactive controls; visible focus states.
- Colour is not the sole carrier of meaning; contrast targets AA.
- Text alternatives for non-text content; captions where media is used.
- Respect for reduced-motion and colour-scheme preferences.

## Known gaps (stated honestly)

QNSI has not yet completed an independent third-party accessibility audit, and we do not claim a formal VPAT/ACR. Complex data visualisations and some deep-technical evidence pages are the most likely places to fall short of AA today. We are not going to claim conformance we have not tested.

If you need a formal conformance report for a procurement process, contact us and we will tell you exactly where we stand.

## Report a barrier

If any part of QNSI is not usable for you, tell us at qnsi-support@heossi.com and describe the barrier and the assistive technology you use. We treat accessibility defects as defects.
