## Our commitments

- We identify HEOSSI or QNSI and provide a functional contact or unsubscribe method in marketing communications.
- We honour valid opt-out requests within the period required by applicable law and do not charge for opting out.
- We do not use address-harvesting or dictionary-attack techniques to obtain recipients.
- For covered marketing to Singapore telephone numbers, we check the Do Not Call Registry or rely on a lawful exception or clear consent in evidential form.
- Transactional, security, billing, and service messages are not marketing and may still be sent where necessary to provide or protect the service.

## Preferences and complaints

Use the unsubscribe control in the message or contact qnsi-legal@heossi.com. Withdrawing marketing consent does not close your account or stop essential service notices.
